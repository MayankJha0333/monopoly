Rent Rush — free online property trading board game

Roll the dice, buy streets, build houses and collect rent from everyone who lands on them. Rent Rush is a free multiplayer board game that runs in your browser — no download, no install.

  • Quick match: four players, about ten minutes, starts in seconds.
  • Private table: share a code and play with friends.
  • Full 3D island board, with a 2D board on slower devices.
  • Trade streets, build houses and hotels, mortgage, and bankrupt your rivals.
  • Play as a guest, or make an account to keep your level, coins and leaderboard place.
